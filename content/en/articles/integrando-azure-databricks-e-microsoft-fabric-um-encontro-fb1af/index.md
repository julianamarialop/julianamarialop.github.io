In this first article, we'll tackle a crucial topic that often comes up in conversations with our clients: integrating Microsoft Azure Databricks with Microsoft Fabric. Both platforms are leaders in their respective fields. [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) is outstanding at scaling data engineering, data science and machine learning workloads. [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/), in turn, stands out for its simplicity and self-service features, making it ideal for a wide range of data applications. The question we're often asked is: how do you integrate these two powerful tools efficiently?

### Integration Approaches

Today there are several options to consider for this integration (keeping in mind that [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/) is constantly being updated, which means we'll soon have even more possibilities).

### Adding Reporting and Analytics Layers

One way to improve a [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/)-enabled architecture is by adding a reporting and analytics layer. This enables more effective visualization of the processed data.

### Integration with OneLake

Another approach is to complement the architecture by incorporating a OneLake gold layer. We can also consider having [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) write all of its data directly to OneLake. Although this practice isn't generally recommended, it's important to discuss its aspects to understand when it can be useful.

### Extending with V-ORDERED

We can also extend [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) by adding a V-ORDERED-enabled consumption layer. This can improve data processing efficiency in both [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) and [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/), adding extra components that can make a difference in performance.

### But before wrapping up, I'd like to answer the following question: Why combine the two platforms?

Organizations choose to integrate [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) and [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/) because of the unique benefits this combination delivers.

[Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) is a powerful and versatile platform designed for data processing, analytics and data science. Widely adopted by companies of all sizes, [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/), created by the developers of Spark, is highly valued for its ability to manage complex workloads at large scale. With a long track record of success, it has established itself as a reliable and efficient tool for data engineers.

[Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/), on the other hand, stands out for its simplicity and ease of use. Launched in 2023 and evolved from Microsoft Power BI, it offers a smooth transition for users who are used to Microsoft Power BI. Its intuitive interface, built-in self-service features and seamless integration with Microsoft Office 365 make [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/) especially attractive for the corporate environment. Designed to democratize access to data and remove barriers to use, it presents itself as an accessible and efficient solution.

So combining [Microsoft Azure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft-azure/) [Databricks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/databricks/) with [Microsoft Fabric](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoftfabric/) offers a robust, complete solution that meets both the technical and the business needs of organizations, which explains its growing popularity.
