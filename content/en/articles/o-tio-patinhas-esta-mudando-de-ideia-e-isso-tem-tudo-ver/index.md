For decades, Scrooge McDuck was the ultimate symbol of concentrated wealth. Owner of a colossal fortune, he preferred to keep his money in a giant vault where he could literally swim in gold coins. Only he had access. Only he made the decisions. And he firmly believed that to protect his fortune, he had to keep it isolated from the world.

But what if Scrooge McDuck lived in 2025?

Maybe he'd realize that his keep-it-all-to-myself model is becoming outdated. And that, instead of a locked vault, he could use technology to multiply value, create opportunities and still keep everything safe. That's where **real estate tokenization** comes in, along with a powerful data platform like **Databricks**.

---

### From sole owner to collective investor: the new way to share wealth

Scrooge always believed real estate was a solid way to hold and grow his wealth. And he wasn't wrong. But until recently, investing in real estate was a privilege reserved for those with a lot of capital, time and tolerance for red tape.

With tokenization, that changes completely.

Real estate tokenization is the process of turning a physical asset, such as a building or a plot of land, into small digital pieces called **tokens**. Each token represents a real fraction of the property and can be bought by anyone, starting at a hundred reais. It's as if Scrooge decided to split his vault into little digital coins and let other people invest in them too, with a share of the profits.

---

### A ledger nobody can scribble over: the magic of blockchain

If Scrooge was obsessed with control and precise records, he'd be impressed by how **blockchain** works. It's a kind of public digital ledger where every transaction is recorded in an encrypted, immutable way.

Picture Scrooge's old cash book, where he wrote down every cent with a fountain pen, being replaced by a network of computers that records every movement with mathematical precision and no way to alter it. That's what guarantees the security and trust behind tokenization.

There's no longer any need to trust third parties or paperwork. The technology itself guarantees that everything is right, something that even Scrooge, suspicious as ever, would learn to value.

---

### When investing is no longer just for people with underground vaults

The big change tokenization brings is the democratization of real estate investing. A market that used to require high entry amounts now lets ordinary people invest with little money, diversify their assets and even earn passive income, like little Scrooges in the making.

The idea that "either you own a whole property or you own nothing" is being replaced by a model that is more accessible, more digital and far more inclusive.

---

### The vault has already started to open: real examples in Brazil

Even if Scrooge were reluctant at first, he couldn't ignore the results of some Brazilian platforms already applying this new logic:

- **Netspaces**: tokenizes properties in Brazilian cities and has already launched projects with Housi, giving access to fractions of modern apartments.
- **Ribus**: bets on tokens with governance, where investors have an active say in managing the properties, something Scrooge, always calling all the shots, might take a while to accept.
- **Propriedade Digital**: is opening the door for new investor profiles to take part in this market, safely and simply.
- **Housi**: turns housing into a service and into a fractional investment asset.

These companies show that the future of the real estate market is already underway and that you no longer need to inherit a gold mine to get started.

---

### The data Scrooge doesn't see, but that moves invisible fortunes

Tokenizing a property isn't just a technological or legal matter. It's also a matter of **data**, lots of data. Information about location, the market, contracts, transactions, investor preferences and regulatory compliance needs to be analyzed and processed in real time.

If Scrooge always hired accountants to handle the paperwork, today he'd need a far more robust platform to keep up with this new volume of information. That's where **Databricks** comes in.

---

### When gold stops shining and data becomes worth more

Databricks works like the brain behind the modern vault. It brings together, organizes and analyzes all the data involved in tokenization, and turns it into practical intelligence.

With it, you can:

- Understand investor behavior and predict trends;
- Evaluate the performance of different properties and suggest which are most viable for tokenization;
- Generate real-time dashboards on liquidity, risk and profitability;
- Ensure the traceability and governance needed to meet the requirements of bodies like the CVM (Brazil's securities regulator).

If Scrooge's coin vault was the symbol of absolute control, Databricks is the new wealth management instrument, only based on data, machine learning and predictive insight.

### The old vault still has value, but the new one is networked

At the end of the day, Scrooge might still keep a few coins as souvenirs, framed on the wall. But if he wants to keep growing, he'll have to accept that **opening the vault**, with security, strategy and intelligence, isn't losing value. It's creating new ways to prosper.

And that's only possible once you understand that blockchain and tokenization change the rules of the game. And that platforms like Databricks let you play with an edge.
