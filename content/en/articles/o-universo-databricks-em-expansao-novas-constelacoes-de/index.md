Like a living, pulsing galaxy, every month brings new "constellations" of features, integrations, and acquisitions that light the way for data professionals and widen the horizons of what's possible with data and artificial intelligence.

May 2025 was a particularly busy month in this technological cosmos. We witnessed the birth of several important "constellations" that are redefining the platform's horizon and opening up new possibilities for organizations looking to extract value from their data. From the billion-dollar acquisition of a promising startup to new tools for marketers, the Databricks universe gained new points of light.

In this article, we'll explore the five main "constellations" that appeared this month, each bringing its own brightness and influence to the Databricks ecosystem. Get ready for a trip through the outer space of data, where each new release represents a significant step forward in the mission to democratize data analytics and artificial intelligence.

### The Neon Supernova (The Neon Acquisition)

At the heart of the Databricks universe, a supernova has just exploded, releasing transformative energy across the whole ecosystem. On May 14, Databricks announced its intention to acquire Neon, a leading serverless Postgres technology company, in a deal valued at approximately US$ 1 billion.

This supernova isn't impressive just for its size, but mainly for the power it adds to the Databricks universe. Neon developed a serverless PostgreSQL database technology that promises to bring speed and flexibility to production workloads. By integrating this technology into its platform, Databricks significantly strengthens its capabilities for developers and AI agents, enabling more robust and efficient AI systems.

Like every supernova that reshapes the space around it, this acquisition will also bring structural changes. Neon's 140 employees will join Databricks once the deal closes, expected on July 31, 2025, at the end of the second quarter. This merger of talent promises to accelerate innovation around serverless PostgreSQL and break down data silos, creating a more unified environment for analytics and AI applications.

### The Marketing Galaxy (Data Intelligence for Marketing)

A new galaxy has just formed in the Databricks universe, with multiple systems orbiting in harmony around a powerful core. Data Intelligence for Marketing, launched on May 14, arrives as a unified solution that promises to revolutionize how marketing teams use data and AI.

This bright galaxy brings all customer and campaign data together in one place, letting marketers get a complete view of their initiatives. Like interconnected stars, the data flows and communicates, revealing patterns and insights that used to stay hidden in the shadows of information silos.

What makes this galaxy even more fascinating are its connections to other systems. Data Intelligence for Marketing comes with ready-made integrations with partners such as Adobe, Braze, and Salesforce, creating a truly interconnected star system. These integrations let marketing teams improve segmentation, personalize experiences in real time, and make decisions based on hard data, not gut feeling. Like a galaxy that shines with its own light, this solution lights the way to next-generation customer experiences.

### The Interstellar Bridge (Native Azure Databricks Connector in Azure AI Foundry)

In the vast universe of data, bridges that connect different regions are as valuable as they are rare. The new native Azure Databricks connector in Azure AI Foundry is one of those interstellar bridges, letting energy (data) flow between different dimensions of the technological cosmos.

This newly built bridge specifically connects Databricks AI/BI Genie with Azure AI Foundry, creating a direct path for data to flow between these two powerful systems. Like a well-planned cosmic route, this connection lets organizations build data-aware AI agents across the entire company, without navigating complex routes or building custom solutions.

The benefits of this interstellar bridge are immense. Organizations can now create richer, more contextual AI experiences, where agents have direct access to the relevant corporate data. Imagine virtual assistants that don't just answer generic questions but can access and analyze your company's specific data to deliver personalized, actionable insights. This bridge doesn't just shorten distances: it creates possibilities that once seemed to belong only to the realm of science fiction.

### The Automation Planetary System (New Databricks Job Activity in Azure Data Factory)

Every well-organized universe needs planetary systems where each celestial body follows its orbit in a coordinated, predictable way. The new Databricks Job activity in Azure Data Factory, announced on May 14, creates exactly this kind of system for automating data flows.

Like planets following their orbits in perfect harmony, Azure Data Factory now supports orchestrating Databricks Jobs, letting organizations schedule and automate Databricks workflows directly from the familiar ADF interface. This integration creates a solar system of automation where each job (planet) follows its predetermined path, interacting with other elements of the system when needed.

The beauty of this planetary system lies in its ability to unify different Azure services in a single orbit of automation. Data teams can now build end-to-end pipelines that combine Databricks processing with other Azure Data Factory activities, without switching between different interfaces or writing complex integration scripts. Like an astronomer who can finally map an entire star system, data engineers now have complete visibility and control over their processing flows.

### The Evolutionary Asteroid Belt (Liquibase Pro Extension for Databricks Integration)

Between the larger planets of a solar system, we often find asteroid belts: collections of smaller bodies that, together, play a crucial role in the system's stability. The new Liquibase Pro Extension for Databricks integration arrives as an evolutionary asteroid belt, protecting and organizing the data space around it.

This extension brings modern change management to the Databricks environment, letting teams control schema and data changes more efficiently and safely. Like asteroids moving in predictable patterns, database changes can now be tracked, versioned, and deployed in a controlled way, reducing risk and increasing reliability.

For teams working with DevOps and CI/CD, this asteroid belt offers protection against catastrophic collisions (unplanned or conflicting changes) and provides a clear path for the continuous evolution of data systems. The integration lets developers and data engineers adopt "database as code" practices, treating schema changes with the same rigor and automation they apply to application code. Like a self-organizing system, this extension helps keep order even in complex, constantly changing data environments.
